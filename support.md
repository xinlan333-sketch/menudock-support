# MenuDock Support

MenuDock is a macOS menu bar utility for launching and switching
between applications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add an application?

Right-click any application icon managed by MenuDock and choose
“Add Application…”.

On first launch, MenuDock starts with an empty list and opens Manage
Applications so you can add your own apps. If all apps are unchecked or the
list is empty, the MenuDock menu bar icon provides access to Manage
Applications and the shared right-click menu.

### Why is there no MenuDock icon in the Dock?

MenuDock runs in the menu bar and does not display a Dock icon.
Opening MenuDock again displays its menu bar menu. Choose Manage
Applications to edit your list, or Settings to adjust preferences.

### How do I remove an application?

Choose Manage Applications from the right-click menu and remove its entry.
Existing saved lists are kept when you update MenuDock.

This does not uninstall or quit the application.

### What should I do if an application will not launch?

Confirm that the application is still installed on your Mac
and can be opened normally from Finder.

If it has been moved or reinstalled, remove its old entry
from MenuDock and add it again.

### How do I quit MenuDock?

Right-click any application icon managed by MenuDock
and choose “Quit MenuDock”.

### Are tips required?

No. Support MenuDock offers an optional, repeatable tip through Apple
In-App Purchase. It does not unlock features and is not a subscription.
The current local price appears before you choose to purchase.

### How do I enable launch at login?

Open Settings and enable launch at login. If macOS asks for approval,
follow the prompt in system settings.
