# MenuDock Privacy Policy

Developer: HUAPH  
Contact: xinlan3721@gmail.com

## Scope

This policy applies to MenuDock for macOS.

## Data Collection

MenuDock does not collect or transmit user data to the developer.
It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking tools, or
third-party SDKs. No account registration is required.

## Local Data Processing

To launch and switch between applications and provide its settings,
MenuDock processes the names, bundle identifiers, locations, icons,
and display order of applications selected by the user.

MenuDock also saves preferences such as interface language, theme,
and icon contraction settings.

This information is processed and stored locally on your Mac.
MenuDock does not upload it to the developer or third parties.

MenuDock does not read documents, chat history, or other content
inside the applications it launches.

## Optional Tips and Apple Purchases

Optional, repeatable tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. A tip
does not unlock features and is not a subscription. Apple handles payment
and account information under its own privacy policy; MenuDock does not
receive your payment card details or App Store account password.

MenuDock uses Apple-verified transaction information to acknowledge a
successful tip and complete the transaction. It saves only a local
true/false acknowledgment preference, not receipts or a transaction history,
and does not send transaction information to a developer-operated server.

## Launch at Login

If you enable launch at login, macOS manages the registration through its
login item service. You can change it in MenuDock Settings or macOS settings.

## Custom Language Packs

Custom language packs are read and stored locally.
MenuDock does not upload them.

## Managing Your Data

You can remove entries in Manage Applications and change preferences in
MenuDock Settings. Removing an entry does not uninstall or quit
the corresponding application.

Quitting MenuDock does not automatically delete saved application
lists or preferences.

For instructions on removing all local configuration data,
please contact the developer using the email address below.

## Contacting the Developer

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address,
message content, and attachments you choose to provide.
We use this information only to respond to your inquiry
or support request.

Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

## Third-Party Applications and Services

Applications launched through MenuDock are governed by their
respective developers' privacy policies.

The hosting service for this policy webpage and the email
service used for correspondence are also governed by their
respective privacy policies.

## Policy Updates

If MenuDock's data practices change, we will update this policy
and the App Store privacy disclosures as applicable.
